## The visual promise

MYTHBEASTS CLASH is a premium-feeling mythological action game about original beast champions fighting across immense lost worlds shaped by a primordial catastrophe. It combines the danger and texture of mythic dark fantasy, believable creature anatomy, monumental ancient environments and spectacular elemental powers.

This is a coherent hybrid, not four competing styles. Dark fantasy establishes mood; beastborn realism establishes character credibility; lost-world scale establishes place; elemental cinema delivers combat spectacle.

## Non-negotiable visual pillars

1. **Creature-first characters:** believable anatomy, species-specific movement, expressive faces and distinctive silhouettes. Armor enhances anatomy rather than replacing it.
2. **Monumental worlds:** large-scale architecture, layered geography, environmental storytelling and distant landmarks. The playable combat plane stays readable.
3. **Cinematic elemental power:** fire, frost, solar energy, storm and primordial effects have distinct visual languages, strong timing and controlled brightness.
4. **Ancient material history:** weathered stone, forged metal, worn leather, mineral deposits, carved symbols, relics and believable damage.
5. **Isometric readability:** silhouettes, attack tells, weapon arcs, hit reactions and floor hazards remain clear on a phone.
6. **Premium restraint:** detail is purposeful; avoid noisy micro-detail, plastic materials, generic sci-fi interfaces, excessive glow and clutter.

## Atmosphere balance

Each scene should contain all three ingredients, with the mix adjusted to its identity:
- **Dark/mysterious:** shadow, tension, ruin, void, weather.
- **Ancient/legendary:** sacred geometry, monuments, relics, history.
- **Elemental/cinematic:** controlled light, particles, environmental reactions and high-impact moments.

A scene may emphasize one pillar, but should not lose the other two entirely.

## Character roster identity

- **Vyrak:** draconic ember bruiser; basalt scales, powerful tail, heavy cleaver and heat beneath the skin.
- **Frostfang:** lupine ice predator; dense pale fur, digitigrade sprint anatomy, glacial talons and a low hunting stance.
- **Solarius:** avian solar guardian; feathered/armored silhouette, spear and shield, poised movement and restrained radiant light.
- **Raijin:** broad storm beast titan; horned crown, heavy forearms, weathered iron and explosive lightning movement.
- **Atrox Nefas:** asymmetrical primordial sovereign; void plates, broken crown, unnatural stillness and environmental distortion.

## World identity

- **Ruined Sanctuary:** first production benchmark; broken monumental temple, guardian effigies, flagstones, braziers, crystals and distant monolith skyline.
- **Frozen Dominion:** glacial citadel, broken bridges, frozen waterfalls and wind-carved stone.
- **Ashen Throne:** volcanic fortress, basalt terraces, obsidian ruins and distant lava falls.
- **Celestial Expanse:** high-altitude sanctuary, broken celestial architecture, cloud depth and suspended fragments.

Environment production standards live in `ENVIRONMENT-ASSET-PIPELINE.md`.

## Quality bar — what is rejected

- Generic humanoid mannequins with creature helmets.
- Identical body plans differentiated only by color or armor.
- Robotic, weightless or skating animation.
- Toy-like plastic surfaces and featureless glossy armor.
- Empty flat arenas, circular boxing-ring layouts or sparse placeholder scenery presented as final art.
- Constant neon outlines, uncontrolled bloom or VFX that hide gameplay.
- Concept art or greybox geometry represented as a finished in-game asset.

## Production truth

Current procedural geometry and proxy fighters are development scaffolding. The final art target requires original sculpted models, production rigs, authored animation, PBR materials, authored environment assets, mobile optimization and in-game review. Do not mark a visual milestone complete until the asset passes its acceptance gate on the target camera and Android hardware.
